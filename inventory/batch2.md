These three should be prioritized for the next deep-extraction pass — they are the closest thing to genuine TOPIK exam material in this batch.
- **Off-topic / not a Korean-learning resource**: `혼자 있고 싶은데 외로운 건 싫어.pdf` is a translated pop-psychology book, unrelated to TOPIK prep or language pedagogy. Recommend excluding from the content pipeline unless repurposed as raw advanced-reading text.
- **Likely incomplete file**: `548194904-Korean-Book.pdf` is only 17 pages despite its own table of contents implying 100+ pages of content — this is a preview/sample excerpt, not the full book. Flag before relying on it for anything beyond the hangul intro.
- **Unicode filename tooling issues**: `663178932-TOPIK-MASTER-TOPIK-2-해설집.pdf` and `혼자 있고 싶은데 외로운 건 싫어.pdf` broke both the `pdftotext` CLI (mangled byte encoding) and the Read tool's direct PDF path (file-not-found) when passed by name. Both were recoverable via directory-listing prefix match + pymupdf rendering — worth remembering for future batches with Korean filenames.
- **Environment note**: the Read tool's built-in PDF-to-image rendering depends on `pdftoppm` (poppler-utils), which is **not installed** in this environment (only `pdftotext.exe` is present in the mingw64 bin). All visual sampling in this batch was done by installing `pymupdf` via pip and rendering pages to PNG manually, then reading those PNGs. Two files (`627905880-...BTS-Book-1.pdf` at 362MB and `534567727-Korean-Picture-Dictionary...pdf` at 128MB) also exceeded the Read tool's 100MB direct-PDF-read limit and required the same pymupdf workaround.
- **Series siblings, not duplicates**: `vitamin_4.pdf` and `524755915-Vitamin-Korean-5.pdf` are adjacent volumes (levels 4 and 5) of the same "비타민 한국어" series — not duplicate files, but worth extracting together since both contain embedded TOPIK-style drills.
- No byte-for-byte or same-page-count duplicate pairs were found **within this 23-file batch** itself (some exist elsewhere in the Downloads folder, e.g. a "- Copy.pdf" of `232589960-2000-Essential-Korean-Words-for-Beginners.pdf`, but those copies are outside this batch's file list).
